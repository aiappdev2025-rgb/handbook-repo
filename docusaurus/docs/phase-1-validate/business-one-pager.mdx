## Why a One-Pager?

Many products fail because founders skip business model clarity and jump straight into building. They end up with:
- A product nobody wants to pay for
- A product priced wrong
- A product marketed to the wrong audience

The One-Pager forces these decisions upfront.

## The Eight Sections

Your Business One-Pager must cover these sections:

### 1. Problem Statement

What specific problem are we solving? Be precise—vague problems lead to vague solutions.

**Good**: "Real estate agents spend 2+ hours writing each property description, and the results are often generic and uninspiring."

**Bad**: "Real estate is inefficient."

### 2. Solution Overview

How does our product solve the problem? Focus on the mechanism, not features.

### 3. Target Customer Profile

Who exactly is this for? Include:
- Demographics (role, company size, location)
- Psychographics (values, behaviors, preferences)
- Buying behavior (budget authority, purchase triggers)

### 4. Value Proposition

Why choose us over alternatives? Complete this sentence:
> "Unlike [alternative], our product [key differentiator]."

### 5. Pricing Strategy

- Price point
- Model (subscription, usage-based, tiered)
- Justification (based on value, competitors, willingness to pay)

### 6. Go-to-Market Strategy

How will you acquire your first 100 customers?
- Initial acquisition channels
- Launch strategy
- Early traction tactics

### 7. Success Metrics

What does success look like?
- 6-month targets
- 12-month targets
- Key metrics to track

### 8. Key Risks

What could make this fail? How do we mitigate each risk?

## Example Excerpt

Here's a partial example for a real estate description generator:

```markdown
## 1. Problem Statement

Real estate agents spend an average of 45 minutes writing each property
description. Most agents write 3-5 listings per week, consuming 2-4 hours
on a task they find tedious. The resulting descriptions are often generic,
fail to highlight unique selling points, and don't convert browsers into
showing requests.

## 2. Solution Overview

ListingAI generates compelling, unique property descriptions in under
60 seconds. Agents input property details and photos, and receive
professionally written descriptions optimized for buyer psychology
and local market language.

## 5. Pricing Strategy

**Price**: $29/month (Starter), $79/month (Pro), $199/month (Team)
**Model**: Subscription with usage tiers
**Justification**: Agents bill $150-500 per listing. At 4 listings/month,
$29 saves 3+ hours of work—clear ROI at ~$10/hour of time saved.
```
